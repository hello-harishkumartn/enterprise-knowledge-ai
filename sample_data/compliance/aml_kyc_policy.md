# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy

## Purpose

As a regulated financial services company, Acme Financial Services maintains an AML/KYC program to detect and prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and fraud, in compliance with the Bank Secrecy Act and applicable FinCEN regulations.

## Customer Identification Program (CIP)

Before opening any account, Acme FS must collect and verify: full legal name, date of birth, physical address, and a government-issued identification number (SSN or equivalent for individuals; EIN for businesses). Identity is verified through a combination of document review and electronic verification services.

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

All new clients undergo risk-based due diligence at onboarding, categorized as Low, Medium, or High risk based on factors including geography, transaction volume expectations, and business type. High-risk clients (e.g., money service businesses, politically exposed persons) undergo Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), including source-of-funds verification.

## Ongoing Monitoring

Client transactions are continuously monitored by automated systems for patterns consistent with money laundering (structuring, rapid movement of funds, transactions inconsistent with expected account activity). Alerts are reviewed by the AML Operations team within 2 business days of generation.

## Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)

Confirmed suspicious activity must be filed as a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) with FinCEN within 30 calendar days of initial detection (extendable to 60 days if no suspect is identified). SAR filings and their underlying facts are strictly confidential and must never be disclosed to the customer, per federal law ("tipping off" is a criminal offense).

## Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)

Cash transactions exceeding $10,000 in a single business day (including structured smaller transactions where structuring is suspected) require a Currency Transaction Report (CTR) filed within 15 calendar days.

## Sanctions Screening

All new clients and counterparties are screened against OFAC sanctions lists and other applicable watchlists at onboarding and re-screened daily against list updates. A confirmed sanctions match blocks the transaction/account immediately and is escalated to the Compliance Officer.

## Training and Accountability

All client-facing and operations employees complete AML/KYC training within their first week (per the Onboarding Process) and annually thereafter. The Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) Officer holds overall accountability for the AML program and reports program status to the Board quarterly.
