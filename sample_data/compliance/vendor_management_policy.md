# Vendor Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs how Acme Financial Services evaluates, onboards, and monitors third-party vendors and service providers.

## Vendor Risk Tiers

Vendors are classified into three risk tiers at intake:

- **Tier 1 (Critical)**: has access to client financial data or is core to service delivery (e.g., cloud infrastructure, core banking software). Requires annual on-site or virtual security audit.
- **Tier 2 (Significant)**: has access to internal (non-client) data or systems. Requires security review every 2 years.
- **Tier 3 (Low)**: no data access (e.g., office supplies vendor). Requires only standard procurement due diligence.

## Onboarding Requirements

Before a Tier 1 or Tier 2 vendor is engaged, Vendor Management must obtain: a completed security questionnaire, proof of a current SOC 2 Type II report (or equivalent), and a signed Data Processing Agreement. Legal must review and approve the master services agreement before signature.

## Ongoing Monitoring

Tier 1 vendors are reassessed annually and must report any security incident affecting Acme FS data within 24 hours of discovery, per their contractual obligations. Vendor Management maintains a central Vendor Risk Register reviewed quarterly by the Vendor Risk Committee.

## Vendor Access to Systems

Any vendor requiring direct access to Acme FS systems must go through the same access provisioning and MFA requirements defined in the Password and Access Management Policy, and access is automatically reviewed every 90 days alongside employee access reviews.

## Termination and Offboarding

When a vendor relationship ends, Vendor Management must confirm within 10 business days that all Acme FS data has been returned or securely destroyed, and that all vendor system access has been revoked, with written attestation retained per the Data Retention Policy.

## Exceptions

Any exception to onboarding requirements (e.g., engaging a Tier 1 vendor without a current SOC 2 report) requires joint sign-off from the CISO and the Head of Procurement, and must include a remediation timeline.
