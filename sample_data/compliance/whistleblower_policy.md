# Whistleblower Policy

## Purpose

Acme Financial Services encourages employees, contractors, and vendors to report suspected violations of law, regulation, or company policy without fear of retaliation.

## How to Report

Concerns may be reported through any of the following channels:

- Direct manager or HR Business Partner
- Compliance team at compliance@acmefs.com
- The anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199, available 24/7 and operated by an independent third party
- Directly to the General Counsel for concerns involving executive leadership

## Anonymity and Confidentiality

Reports made through the Ethics Hotline may be submitted anonymously. Regardless of channel, Acme FS limits knowledge of a report's details to those with a legitimate need to investigate, consistent with the Data Classification Policy's Confidential handling requirements.

## Non-Retaliation

Acme FS strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, even if the concern is ultimately not substantiated. Retaliation includes termination, demotion, harassment, or any adverse employment action taken because of a good-faith report. Suspected retaliation should be reported immediately through the same channels above and is treated as a serious Code of Conduct violation.

## Investigation Process

All reports are triaged by the Compliance team within 3 business days. Substantive reports are assigned an investigator (from Compliance, HR, or an external firm for executive-level or legally sensitive matters) and are typically resolved within 45 days, though complex investigations may take longer with documented justification.

## Scope of Reportable Concerns

Reportable concerns include, but are not limited to: financial reporting irregularities, insider trading, bribery or corruption, harassment or discrimination, safety violations, and violations of the AML/KYC Policy.

## Board Oversight

The Audit Committee of the Board receives a summary of whistleblower reports and their resolution status quarterly, with immediate notification required for any report involving potential financial statement fraud or executive misconduct.
