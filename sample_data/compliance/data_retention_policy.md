# Data Retention Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines minimum and maximum retention periods for categories of records created or held by Acme Financial Services, balancing regulatory obligations with data minimization principles.

## Retention Schedule

| Record Type | Retention Period | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Client account records | 7 years after account closure | Finance |
| Transaction records | 7 years from transaction date | Finance |
| Employee personnel files | 7 years after termination | HR |
| Payroll records | 7 years | Finance/HR |
| Security incident reports | 7 years | Security |
| Vendor due diligence records | 5 years after contract end | Procurement |
| Marketing communications consent records | 3 years after last contact | Marketing |
| CCTV / physical access logs | 90 days, unless under legal hold | Facilities |
| General internal email | 3 years, then auto-archived | IT |

## Legal Holds

Any record subject to a litigation hold, regulatory inquiry, or audit must be preserved regardless of the standard retention schedule above, until Legal formally releases the hold. Employees are notified of an active legal hold via a Legal Hold Notice and must not delete relevant records.

## Secure Disposal

Records reaching the end of their retention period must be securely destroyed: physical documents are cross-shredded, and electronic records are deleted using a method that prevents recovery, consistent with the Data Classification Policy's handling requirements for Confidential and Restricted data.

## Client Data Deletion Requests

Where required by applicable privacy law, client requests for data deletion are routed to the Data Privacy team, who evaluate the request against regulatory retention requirements (e.g., a 7-year transaction record requirement may override a deletion request) and respond within 30 days.

## Backup Retention

System backups are retained for 90 days on a rolling basis for disaster recovery purposes, separate from the primary retention schedule above, and are not treated as the system of record for retention compliance.

## Responsibility

Each record owner listed in the schedule above is responsible for ensuring records within their domain are retained and disposed of correctly; IT provides the technical tooling (archival, deletion holds) to support compliance.
