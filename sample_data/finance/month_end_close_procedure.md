# Month-End Close Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines the standard timeline and checklist Finance follows to close the books each month, ensuring accurate and timely financial reporting.

## Close Timeline

The month-end close follows a 5-business-day timeline:

- **Day 1**: All subsidiary ledgers (AP, AR, payroll) are locked for the prior month; late invoices must be submitted as exceptions.
- **Day 2**: Accruals and prepaid expense amortization are booked; intercompany transactions are reconciled.
- **Day 3**: Bank and account reconciliations are completed by the Accounting team.
- **Day 4**: Financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow) are drafted and reviewed by the Controller.
- **Day 5**: The CFO reviews and approves final financials; the close is formally declared complete.

## Journal Entry Approval

All manual journal entries over $25,000 require review and approval by the Controller before posting, in addition to standard segregation-of-duties controls described in the Finance Expense Approval Policy.

## Reconciliation Standards

Every balance sheet account must be reconciled monthly with supporting documentation retained per the Data Retention Policy's 7-year transaction record requirement. Reconciling items outstanding more than 60 days must be escalated to the Controller.

## Flux Analysis

The Accounting team prepares a variance ("flux") analysis for any account or expense line that moves more than 10% or $50,000 month-over-month, whichever is lower, with a written explanation attached to the close package.

## Reporting Package

The final close package — financial statements, flux analysis, and a close checklist sign-off — is distributed to department VPs by the end of Day 5 and to the Board ahead of each quarterly Board meeting.

## Year-End Close

The December close follows an extended 10-business-day timeline to accommodate annual audit procedures and does not follow the standard 5-day cycle described above.
