# Finance Expense Approval Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines approval thresholds and controls for company spending, separate from the employee Travel and Expense Policy which covers individual travel reimbursement.

## Approval Thresholds

- Purchases under $1,000: manager approval only.
- Purchases $1,000–$10,000: manager plus department VP approval.
- Purchases $10,000–$100,000: VP approval plus Finance Business Partner sign-off.
- Purchases over $100,000: requires Chief Financial Officer (CFO) approval and, if a new vendor, a completed Vendor Management Policy review.

## Purchase Orders

Any purchase over $5,000 requires a Purchase Order (PO) issued through the Procurement system before the vendor is engaged; retroactive POs are discouraged and require Finance Director sign-off.

## Budget Ownership

Each department has an annual operating budget owned by its VP. Spending against budget is tracked monthly, and any department projected to exceed its annual budget by more than 10% must submit a budget variance explanation to the CFO's office.

## Invoice Processing

Approved invoices are processed for payment within 30 days of receipt (net-30 terms), or per the specific terms negotiated in the vendor contract. Rush payments outside standard terms require Finance Director approval.

## Corporate Credit Cards

Departmental corporate cards have a default limit of $10,000 per month. Limit increases require VP and Finance approval and are reviewed at each fiscal quarter close.

## Segregation of Duties

The person requesting a purchase may not also be the final approver, and the person approving payment may not also reconcile that payment in the general ledger, in line with Acme FS's internal control framework required for SOX-adjacent financial governance.

## Fiscal Year and Budget Cycle

Acme FS's fiscal year runs from January 1 to December 31. Department budgets for the following year must be submitted to Finance by October 15 and are typically approved by the Board by mid-November.
