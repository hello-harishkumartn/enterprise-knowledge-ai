# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Acme Financial Services holds all employees, contractors, and officers to the highest standards of integrity. This Code of Conduct applies to everyone acting on behalf of Acme FS, with no exceptions for seniority.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or family relationship that could reasonably be seen to influence a business decision. Disclosures are made via the Annual Conflict of Interest questionnaire or as soon as a new conflict arises, whichever is sooner. Undisclosed conflicts of interest are treated as a serious violation.

## Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may accept business gifts valued under $100 per year from any single vendor or client. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards, etc.) of any amount must be declined. Any gift over $100 must be reported to the Compliance team and may need to be returned or donated.

## Insider Trading

Because Acme FS is a financial services company, employees with access to material non-public information (MNPI) about clients or partners are strictly prohibited from trading on that information, or "tipping" others. Violations are reported to the SEC and can result in immediate termination and criminal referral.

## Confidential Information

Employees must not disclose confidential client data, trade secrets, or internal financial information outside the Company, including on personal social media, except as required by law or with prior written approval from Legal.

## Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

Acme FS prohibits offering, promising, or giving anything of value to a government official or private party to improperly influence a business decision, in compliance with the FCPA and equivalent international anti-bribery laws.

## Reporting Violations

Employees who suspect a violation of this Code must report it to their manager, the Compliance team (compliance@acmefs.com), or the anonymous Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-0199). Acme FS strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Consequences of Violations

Confirmed violations of this Code of Conduct are addressed under the progressive discipline process described in the Employee Handbook, except that fraud, insider trading, harassment, and safety violations may result in immediate termination.

## Annual Acknowledgment

All employees must complete Code of Conduct training and sign an acknowledgment within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter each January.
