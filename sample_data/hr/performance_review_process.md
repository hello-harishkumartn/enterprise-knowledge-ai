# Performance Review Process

## Review Cycle

Acme Financial Services runs two formal performance review cycles per year: a mid-year check-in in June and an annual review in December. Compensation changes are tied only to the December cycle.

## Rating Scale

Performance is rated on a 5-point scale:

1. Unsatisfactory
2. Needs Improvement
3. Meets Expectations
4. Exceeds Expectations
5. Outstanding

The majority of employees are expected to fall into the "Meets Expectations" or "Exceeds Expectations" categories; ratings of 5 (Outstanding) are capped at 10% of any given team to preserve calibration integrity.

## Self-Assessment and Manager Review

Employees complete a self-assessment in the Performance Portal at least 1 week before their scheduled review meeting. Managers then complete their own written assessment and hold a 1:1 review meeting to discuss both.

## Calibration

Ratings are calibrated across each department by senior leadership before being finalized, to ensure consistency in how ratings are applied across teams.

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

An employee rated "Needs Improvement" or "Unsatisfactory" is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) for 60 days, with clear, measurable goals and check-ins every 2 weeks with their manager and an HR Business Partner. Failure to meet PIP goals may result in termination.

## Promotions

Promotion requests are submitted by managers during the annual review cycle and require approval from the department VP and HR. Off-cycle promotions are only granted for retention risk or a significant, documented change in role scope.

## Peer Feedback

Employees may request 360-degree peer feedback from up to 5 colleagues per cycle through the Performance Portal; peer feedback is anonymized before being shared with the reviewing manager.
