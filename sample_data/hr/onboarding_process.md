# New Employee Onboarding Process

## Pre-Boarding (Before Day 1)

Once an offer is signed, HR creates the onboarding record, which automatically triggers IT equipment provisioning (see IT Helpdesk Procedures) and account creation within 1 business day. New hires receive a welcome email with day-one logistics 5 business days before their start date.

## Day One

New employees complete I-9/right-to-work verification, benefits enrollment elections, and direct deposit setup through the HR portal on their first day. IT distributes the laptop and conducts a 30-minute systems orientation covering VPN, MFA enrollment, and the Acceptable Use Policy.

## First Week

New hires complete mandatory compliance training during their first week: Code of Conduct, Information Security awareness, and (for client-facing roles) AML/KYC basics. Managers assign a peer "onboarding buddy" for the first 30 days.

## 30-60-90 Day Check-ins

As referenced in the Employee Handbook's probationary period section, managers hold structured check-ins at day 30, 60, and 90 covering role clarity, early wins, and any support needed. HR Business Partners join the day-90 check-in.

## Benefits Enrollment

New hires have 30 days from their start date to elect health, dental, and vision benefits, and to set 401(k) contribution elections; absent an election, employees default to no benefits coverage until the next open enrollment period, except for company-paid basic life insurance which is automatic.

## Department-Specific Onboarding

Each department maintains a role-specific onboarding checklist (system access, shadowing schedule, initial training) that supplements this company-wide process; these are owned by department managers, not centrally by HR.

## Completion Tracking

HR tracks onboarding task completion in the HR portal and flags any incomplete mandatory item (e.g., unfinished compliance training) to the employee's manager at the 2-week mark.
