# Travel and Expense Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all Acme Financial Services employees traveling for business purposes and covers pre-trip approval, booking, allowable expenses, and reimbursement.

## Trip Approval

All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager through the Travel Portal before booking. International travel additionally requires approval from the department Vice President.

## Booking and Airfare

Employees must book flights through the corporate travel platform (Concur) whenever available. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted for flights exceeding 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval regardless of flight duration.

## Lodging

Hotel bookings should not exceed $250 per night in standard cost-of-living cities and $350 per night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Singapore, as designated by Finance). Exceptions require manager approval in advance.

## Meals and Per Diem

Employees traveling on company business receive a daily meal per diem of $75 domestically and $95 internationally, covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Per diem is not paid for days when meals are provided at a conference or event.

## Ground Transportation

Ride-share and taxi expenses are reimbursable for travel to/from airports, client sites, and between meetings. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate in effect at the time of travel.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: personal entertainment, in-room movies, spa services, traffic or parking fines, alcohol purchased outside of a client business meal (up to $50 per person with manager pre-approval), and travel companion costs.

## Submitting Expense Reports

Expense reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days of trip completion through the Expense Portal, with itemized receipts attached for any single expense over $25. Reports submitted more than 60 days after the trip may be denied at Finance's discretion.

## Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year are issued a corporate card. The corporate card must not be used for personal expenses; violations may result in card revocation and disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.
