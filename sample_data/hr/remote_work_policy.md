# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility

Roles are classified by the hiring manager and HR as Office-Based, Hybrid, or Fully Remote at the time of hire. Reclassification requests are reviewed twice a year, in January and July.

## Hybrid Work Schedule

Hybrid employees are required to work from an Acme FS office a minimum of 2 days per week, with Tuesday designated as a company-wide in-office collaboration day. The remaining in-office day is scheduled with the employee's manager.

## Fully Remote Employees

Fully remote employees must reside within the same country as their assigned Acme FS office for tax and regulatory reasons, and must attend in-person team gatherings when scheduled, typically quarterly.

## Home Office Stipend

Acme FS provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $500 for new hybrid or remote employees, plus an ongoing $50 per month stipend toward internet and utilities.

## Equipment and Security

Remote employees must use company-issued laptops with full-disk encryption and must connect to internal systems only via the corporate VPN. Use of personal devices to access client data is prohibited under the Information Security Policy.

## Working Hours While Remote

Remote and hybrid employees are expected to maintain the same core collaboration hours (11:00 AM–3:00 PM local time) defined in the Employee Handbook and to keep their calendar status accurate to reflect availability.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from a country other than an employee's country of record requires prior approval from HR and Legal, and is generally limited to 20 business days per calendar year due to tax and employment law constraints.

## Performance Expectations

Remote work arrangements are a privilege tied to sustained performance. A rating below "Meets Expectations" on two consecutive performance reviews may result in a required return to office-based status.
