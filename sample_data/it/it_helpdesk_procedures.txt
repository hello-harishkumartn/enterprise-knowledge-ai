IT HELPDESK PROCEDURES

CONTACTING THE HELPDESK

The IT Helpdesk is available Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–7:00 PM local time, via extension 4357 (HELP), email at helpdesk@acmefs.com, or the self-service portal. Outside these hours, SEV-1 outages are routed to the on-call engineer via PagerDuty.

TICKET PRIORITY LEVELS

P1 (Critical): complete inability to work, or an outage affecting an entire team. Target response: 15 minutes.

P2 (High): significant impairment for one person (e.g., laptop won't boot). Target response: 2 hours.

P3 (Medium): non-blocking issue (e.g., slow performance). Target response: 1 business day.

P4 (Low): general requests (software install, access request). Target response: 3 business days.

NEW HIRE EQUIPMENT

New hire laptops are shipped or ready for pickup 3 business days before the employee's start date, provided the HR onboarding record was created at least 5 business days in advance. Standard equipment includes a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset.

PASSWORD RESETS

Employees can self-service reset their password via the Single Sign-On portal using MFA. If MFA is unavailable, identity must be verified by the Helpdesk via a video call with a government-issued ID before a manual reset is performed.

SOFTWARE REQUESTS

Requests for new software must be submitted through the IT Service Catalog. Standard catalog items are auto-approved; non-catalog software requires manager approval and, for tools touching client data, Security team review, typically completed within 5 business days.

LOANER EQUIPMENT

Employees may request loaner laptops for up to 10 business days while their primary device is repaired. Loaner requests are made through the Helpdesk portal and require manager acknowledgment.

EQUIPMENT RETURN

All company equipment must be returned to IT within 5 business days of an employee's last day. Unreturned equipment may be charged back to the employee's final paycheck where permitted by local law.
