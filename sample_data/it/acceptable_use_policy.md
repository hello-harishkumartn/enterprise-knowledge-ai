# Acceptable Use Policy

## Scope

This policy governs the use of Acme Financial Services computing resources, including laptops, email, internal applications, and internet access, by all employees and contractors.

## Permitted Use

Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited incidental personal use (e.g., checking personal email, brief web browsing) is permitted during breaks, provided it does not violate any other Acme FS policy or consume significant network resources.

## Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited on company systems:

- Accessing, storing, or transmitting illegal content
- Installing unlicensed or unapproved software
- Disabling or circumventing security controls (antivirus, EDR, VPN)
- Using company email for outside business ventures or solicitation
- Sharing account credentials with another person, including coworkers

## Software Installation

Employees may only install software from the approved IT Software Catalog. Requests for additional software must go through the IT Helpdesk and, for tools that touch client data, require Security team review.

## Internet and Email Monitoring

Acme FS reserves the right to monitor use of company systems, including email and internet activity, to the extent permitted by law, for security and compliance purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in company-owned systems.

## Social Media

Employees may identify their employer on personal social media but must include a disclaimer that views expressed are their own, and must never disclose confidential client information, financial results before public release, or internal system details.

## Removable Media

Use of USB drives and other removable media on systems that access Confidential or Restricted data requires prior approval from the Security team and must use company-encrypted devices only.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy are handled under the Code of Conduct's progressive discipline process; deliberate security circumvention may result in immediate termination.
