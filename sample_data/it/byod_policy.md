# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which employees may use personal mobile devices to access limited Acme Financial Services resources.

## Enrollment

Personal devices must be enrolled in the corporate Mobile Device Management (MDM) system before accessing any company resource, including email. Enrollment requires a passcode of at least 6 digits and remote-wipe capability enabled.

## Permitted Access

Enrolled personal devices may access: company email, calendar, and the company directory app. Personal devices may never access client financial data, the core banking platform, or any system classified Confidential or Restricted under the Data Classification Policy — those require a company-issued, encrypted laptop.

## Data Separation

The MDM profile creates a separate, encrypted container for company data on the personal device. Employees must not copy company email attachments into personal apps or personal cloud storage.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen enrolled device must be reported to the IT Helpdesk within 1 hour of discovery so the company data container can be remotely wiped. Failure to report in a timely manner is treated as a security incident under the Incident Response Procedures.

## Device Eligibility

Supported personal devices must run a currently-supported OS version (iOS or Android within the last 2 major versions) and receive security patches; devices that fall out of support are automatically unenrolled by the MDM system.

## Reimbursement

Acme FS does not reimburse the purchase of personal devices used under BYOD, but eligible remote/hybrid employees receive the monthly connectivity stipend described in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

## Termination of Enrollment

Upon termination of employment, the MDM profile and all company data are remotely wiped from the personal device within 4 hours, consistent with the offboarding timelines in the Password and Access Management Policy.
