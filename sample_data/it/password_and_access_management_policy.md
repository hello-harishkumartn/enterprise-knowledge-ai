# Password and Access Management Policy

## Account Provisioning

New employee accounts are provisioned by IT within 1 business day of the HR onboarding record being created, following the principle of least privilege: employees receive only the access required for their role.

## Password Standards

Passwords for all Acme FS systems must:

- Be at least 14 characters long
- Include upper case, lower case, a number, and a symbol
- Not reuse any of the previous 10 passwords
- Be changed every 180 days for systems handling Confidential or Restricted data, and every 365 days for all other systems

## Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is mandatory for: VPN access, email, the HR portal, the Finance systems, and any system accessing client financial data. Acme FS uses an authenticator app as the primary MFA method; SMS-based MFA is permitted only as a fallback.

## Privileged Access

Administrator-level access to production systems requires manager and Security team approval, is reviewed quarterly, and is automatically revoked after 90 days of inactivity. All privileged sessions are logged and retained for 1 year.

## Access Reviews

Managers must certify their direct reports' system access entitlements every 90 days through the Access Review Portal. Uncertified access is automatically revoked after a 15-day grace period.

## Offboarding

IT must disable all account access within 4 hours of an employee's last day, as notified by HR through the offboarding workflow. For involuntary terminations, access is disabled at the start of the exit meeting.

## Shared and Service Accounts

Shared logins are prohibited except for designated service accounts, which must be registered with IT, have a named human owner, and use a password vault rather than a static shared password.
