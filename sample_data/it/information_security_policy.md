# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes baseline information security requirements for all Acme Financial Services employees, contractors, and systems, in support of the Company's obligations under GLBA, SOC 2, and applicable financial services regulations.

## Data Classification

All company data must be classified into one of four tiers, as defined in the Data Classification Policy: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Handling requirements scale with sensitivity.

## Password Requirements

All Acme FS accounts require passwords of at least 14 characters, including a mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 180 days for systems handling Confidential or Restricted data, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems accessing client financial data.

## Device Security

All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption, endpoint detection and response (EDR) software, and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices ("BYOD") may only access email and calendar, never client financial data, per the BYOD Policy.

## Acceptable Use

Company systems are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or violate any other Acme FS policy, as detailed in the Acceptable Use Policy.

## Third-Party and Vendor Access

Any vendor requiring access to Acme FS systems or data must complete a security review coordinated by the Vendor Management team and sign a Data Processing Agreement before access is granted, per the Vendor Management Policy.

## Security Awareness Training

All employees must complete annual security awareness training, including phishing simulation exercises. Employees who fail 2 consecutive phishing simulations are required to complete supplemental training within 10 business days.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident — lost device, phishing click, suspicious account activity — must be reported immediately to the Security Operations team at security@acmefs.com or extension 4400. Detailed handling steps are defined in the Incident Response Procedures document.

## Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and must be time-bound with a documented compensating control.
